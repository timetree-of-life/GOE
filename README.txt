Methods for ESL-PSC modeling:

For each analysis,  100 datasets were generated by randomly sampling one species per clade, yielding 8-species datasets in which the presence of aerobic respiration in a taxon was encoded as +1 and its absence as −1. For each dataset, 10 models were trained using evenly spaced values of the L1 regularization parameter (0.01–0.99 on a logarithmic scale), and the best-fit model was selected (Allard, et al., 2025). Model accuracy was evaluated on taxa not used for training. Balanced accuracy was computed as the mean of sensitivity and specificity. Values shown represent averages across 100 ESL-PSC models. The accuracy of models using 50 markers as well as all the markers (5,641 Pfams) was comparable (96.3% and 95.8%, respectively) to that of the noncanonical markers (95.8%) as well as Liao et al. (93%).

Data files:

SeqIDs_for_4_deep_divergences.csv
List of SeqIDs associated with each of the four pre-GOE divergences between aerobic and anaerobic bacteria which formed the basis of the ESL-PSC models.

SeqIDs_for_reversals.csv
List of SeqIDs associated with phenotypic reversals wherin aerobic respiration was lost among members of an aerobic clade.
